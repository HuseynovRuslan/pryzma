## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-end civic, editorial, and institutional presence tailored for Azerbaijani creative competitions and architectural initiatives. It reflects regional permanence, cultural intellectualism, and architectural rigor.

### Personality & Audience
- **Target Audience:** Designers, visual artists, architects, researchers, and cultural institutions across Azerbaijan and the broader regional creative sector.
- **Brand Attributes:** Authoritative, civic-minded, timeless, pristine, and culturally grounded.
- **Emotional Response:** Inspires quiet confidence, institutional trust, civic pride, and academic rigor—deliberately steering clear of gamified prize wheels, high-frequency gambling aesthetics, or speculative tech noise.

### Design Movement
- **Minimalist Editorial Modernism with Architectural Tactility:** Grounded in grid precision, stark structural hairline borders, refined tabular metrics, and ample breathing room reminiscent of European architectural monographs and national cultural foundations.

## Colors

The color palette draws from the natural minerals, limestone masonry, and sun-baked soils of the Absheron peninsula and Əmircan stone heritage.

- **Primary (`#0E1F23`):** Deep petrol slate—providing authoritative contrast without the harsh artificial coldness of pure black.
- **Secondary (`#C26D45`):** Warm Absheron terracotta—used for focal badges, verified competition banners, and selected active states.
- **Tertiary (`#C99A45`):** Architectural amber—utilized for prize distinctions, historical dates, and contextual emblems.
- **Neutral (`#111827`):** Charcoal ink for ultra-crisp typographic body hierarchies.

### Surface System
The system rejects stark artificial whites in global canvases, opting instead for `#F7F7F5` and `#F1F2EE` surfaces accented with crisp `#FFFFFF` floating structural cards and `#E2E5DD` hairline separators.

## Typography

Typography balances Swiss editorial structure with contemporary technical geometry. 

- **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk):** Provides structured geometric weights reminiscent of technical briefs and architectural journals. Tight tracking creates punchy, monumental statements in Azerbaijani titles (e.g., "Bir mövzu, min baxış").
- **Body Text (Hanken Grotesk):** Delivers clean readability with an understated European modernist posture, remaining clear and transparent across high-volume jury requirements and competition briefs.
- **Tabular Figures:** Timers, monetary prize metrics, and jury numbers strictly demand monospaced or tabular proportional numerals (`tnum`) for absolute alignment across countdown clocks and financial awards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by a strict 12-column architectural grid with broad breathing corridors that prioritize editorial presence over condensed commercial patterns.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `gutter` 1.5rem (24px), page canvas margin `3rem` (48px), max-width bounded at 1320px for content discipline.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `gutter` 1.25rem (20px), canvas margin `2rem` (32px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-sm` 1rem (16px), canvas margin `1.25rem` (20px).

### Spacing Philosophy
Vertical rhythm is spaced deliberately in multiples of 8px. Major page sections feature large demarcations (`2.5rem` to `4rem`) separated by hairline architectural rules (`1px solid #E2E5DD`) to create structured, museum-catalog divisions.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through **structural borders and tonal planes** rather than layered drop shadows, maintaining an authentic, print-editorial texture.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F7F7F5` matte paper backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Tier):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a crisp 1px `#E2E5DD` border. Shadows are absent or limited to a feather-light ambient contact shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(14, 31, 35, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Competition Cards):** Subtle micro-elevation `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(14, 31, 35, 0.06)`, with border transitioning to `#0E1F23`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `box-shadow: 0 20px 48px -8px rgba(14, 31, 35, 0.12)`, anchored by a 1px border. Backdrop uses a subtle frosted matte veil (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(247, 247, 245, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary employs **Soft architectural precision (level 1)**.

- Standard buttons, input elements, card containers, and countdown modules adhere to `0.25rem` (4px) radii.
- Secondary badges, tags, and small metadata capsules use `0.25rem` to maintain an architectural blueprint feel.
- High curvature (pills or circles) is strictly reserved for user avatars and circular step/sequence markers. This restraint avoids whimsical consumer software tropes and honors physical stone and paper geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#0E1F23`, text `#FFFFFF`, 4px radius, 12px 24px padding. Sharp hover transition shifting to `#C26D45` with no movement. Font: Space Grotesk SemiBold.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, 1px solid `#0E1F23`, text `#0E1F23`. Hover: Background `#F1F2EE`.
- **Ghost / Editorial Link:** Underlined with 1.5px solid `#0E1F23`, offset 6px. Clean, museum-style interaction.

### Cards (Competition Entries)
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E2E5DD` perimeter.
- Header image adheres to a disciplined 4:3 or 16:9 ratio with zero corner bleed and a 1px bottom border separator.
- Body content maintains structured padding (`1.5rem`), organizing category chips, title, timeline metrics, and prize purses in distinct typographic blocks.

### Countdown Timer Tiles
- Structured as individual grid cells with 1px `#E2E5DD` borders on `#F1F2EE` background.
- Numeric characters displayed in `label-tabular` (22px Space Grotesk) with monotonic letter spacing, paired with 10px uppercase tracking labels ("GÜN", "SAAT", "DƏQİQƏ").

### Badges & Category Chips
- Compact, flat capsules: 4px radius, padding 4px 10px.
- Subtle stone tint `#F1F2EE` with `#0E1F23` label typography for standard metadata.
- Terracotta `#C26D45` low-opacity wash (10% alpha) with solid `#C26D45` text for urgent phases and active submission deadlines.

### Input Fields
- Flat `#FFFFFF` interior with 1px `#E2E5DD` borders, 4px radius, 12px 16px padding.
- Focused state avoids generic colored glow halos; it snaps decisively to a 1.5px solid `#0E1F23` stroke.

### Lists & Tables
- Clean tabular rules utilizing 1px `#E2E5DD` dividers.
- Row hover states subtle `#F1F2EE` tone shifts. Generous vertical cell padding (16px) for an unhurried, institutional review experience.
